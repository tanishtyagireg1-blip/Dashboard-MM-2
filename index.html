Assumptions and limits

Synthetic panel and hypothetical parameters are illustrative and allowed by the WAI guidelines; they show direction and sensitivity, not a forecast of actual Reliance results. Real anchors: IMARC market size (Rs 271.7 bn, 2026E; 11.29% CAGR), Kwality Wall's Q1 FY27 gross margin 45.6%, Census 2011 urbanisation, state per-capita NSDP (Economic Survey 2023-24). The availability effect assumes a log-availability coefficient of 1; at 0.5 it roughly halves.
